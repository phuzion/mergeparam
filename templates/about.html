About mergeparam

Why

The RfC “Alma mater vs Education in Infoboxes”, closed on {{ config.RFC_CLOSED }}, found consensus to merge |alma_mater= into |education=. The affected infoboxes now flag |alma_mater= as deprecated. Where an infobox has only |alma_mater=, the fix is a simple rename. Where it has both, someone has to decide how to combine them, as several participants in the RfC pointed out.

Before suggesting a change, the tool checks that the infobox actually displays |education=, so no information is hidden by the edit.

Your edits are your edits

This tool never edits Wikipedia. It sends the suggested text to Wikipedia's own “Show changes” screen, and you decide whether to publish it from your account. You're responsible for each edit, as with any other edit you make. Please look at every diff, and edit at a human pace. See assisted editing and WP:MEATBOT.

If Wikipedia shows an edit conflict, the article changed after the tool loaded it. Skip it or fix it by hand.

Privacy

The tool doesn't log in to anything and doesn't store anything about you. If you change a setting, it's saved in a cookie on your browser that holds only that setting. Your browser talks only to Toolforge and Wikimedia sites. When you publish, you do so directly on Wikipedia under Wikipedia's own privacy policy.
