Help merge |alma_mater= into |education=

In July 2026 an RfC agreed to stop using the |alma_mater= infobox parameter and use |education= instead. Thousands of biographies still need the change, and many of them need a human to decide how the two values fit together.

How it works

  1. We pick an article and suggest the change. If both parameters are filled in, you decide what the combined |education= should say.
  2. You review and publish it on Wikipedia. The tool opens Wikipedia's own “Show changes” screen. You publish the edit from your own account; this tool never edits for you.
  3. On to the next one. Skip anything you're unsure about.

Log in to Wikipedia first so the edit is credited to your account. If you're logged out, Wikipedia will save it under a temporary account instead.
